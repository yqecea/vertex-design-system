§ 06 RAW CARBON TEXTURE

The raw carbon texture simulates powder-coated aluminum or cast iron surfaces. Applied via SVG feTurbulence noise filter at 0.9 baseFrequency with 3% opacity overlay.

Use on CASTING material surfaces. The noise adds tactile depth without competing with content. Never stack with other textures.
